## 2. Displacement check

16 x pi/4 x 86.0^2 x 86.0 mm3 = **7992.92 cm3**; published 7993 cm3; deviation -0.0011 %.
Per cylinder 499.56 cm3. The commonly reported 86 x 86 mm is therefore consistent and is adopted.
Because the VR bores are offset from the crank axis by 12.5 mm, a stroke of exactly 86.0 mm needs a crank
throw of 42.8680 mm rather than 43.0 mm.

firing intervals found numerically are 45.000-45.000 deg. The published order is therefore
exactly reproducible with one rod of each bank on each of eight crankpins. Pin phases (deg, pins 1-8):

The 23.13 deg stagger between odd and even pins is the VR correction (the VW W12 has 21.833 deg, SSP 248).
